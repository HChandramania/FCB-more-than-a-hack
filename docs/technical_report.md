# Ball possession from automatic tracking

Technical report for More Than A Hack 2027. Team: **Visca Barca**. Audit date: 17 November 2026.
Deliver this Markdown document as `Visca Barca.md`. No competition performance is claimed.

## Task and possession semantics

The system assigns home possession (H), away possession (A), dead ball (D), or abstention (X)
to each tactical-video frame. A brief opponent touch does not necessarily transfer possession.
The implemented control rule keeps the previous owner for opponent runs shorter than two seconds;
a run of at least two seconds transfers possession from its start. Dead ball and X split live runs.
This is an operational approximation of controlled possession, not a claim to identify intent.

## Inputs and provenance

Only the organizer's tactical ATD TXT and its original XML metadata are used at prediction time.
XML supplies the ordered slot-team mapping. Official `games.py` supplies pitch dimensions and half
boundaries. ATD frame IDs map to submission frames by subtracting one. Broadcast assets, video,
Smart Tagging, homography and possession ground truth are not prediction inputs.

## Preprocessing and features

Coordinates are converted from normalized pitch fractions to metres using official pitch dimensions.
Missing observations remain NaN. Interior player-slot gaps up to ten seconds are interpolated;
positions outside the official halves are masked before feature generation. Identity continuity
within a tracking slot is an assumption and a limitation. External halves have a 1,000-frame NaN/X
separator to keep smoothing windows apart.

Signals describe movement, collective shape, attacking direction, ball proximity, touch timing and
ball advance. Attacking direction is inferred algorithmically per half. LightGBM features use
centered windows of 0.5, 1.5, 4.5, 13.5 and 40 seconds plus trend windows of 3 and 9 seconds.
Dead-ball models use player-only signals to reduce reliance on external providers' ball conventions.
The final LightGBM schemas contain 161 dead-ball and 210 team features; TCN schemas contain 23 and
38 base signals respectively. These dimensions passed runtime preflight.

## Classifiers and ensembles

LightGBM binary classifiers estimate P(dead) and P(home | live), with 300 trees, learning rate
0.05, 31 leaves and minimum child samples 200 (`gbm.py:23-25`). Team-swapped training examples and
averaged swapped predictions enforce team symmetry. Training rows are sampled every five frames.
Each external dataset receives equal total sample weight.

TCNs operate at five steps per second. Each has 64 channels and eight residual convolution blocks
with dilations 1 through 128, giving a 511-step receptive field, approximately 102 seconds.
Inputs combine normalized signals and observation masks; paired team statistics share normalization.
Training uses weighted binary cross entropy, Adam, 1,500 updates, 1,024-step crops and dropout 0.1.
Team-change neighborhoods receive triple loss weight. Networks enter evaluation mode at prediction.

P(dead) averages a LightGBM and TCN pair for clean models and an equivalent pair trained with player
dropout, then averages those two pairs: four classifiers. P(home | live) averages LightGBM and a
three-seed TCN mean within each clean/dropout group, then averages the groups: eight classifiers.
The twelve pretrained artifacts are distributed with `models/SHA256SUMS`.

External training includes seeded simulated ball loss and additional copies with 10% player-track
dropout. No fitting or calibration occurs in `scripts/predict.py`.

## Temporal decoding

The three emission probabilities are `(1-P(dead))*P(home|live)`,
`(1-P(dead))*(1-P(home|live))`, and `P(dead)`. Viterbi decoding penalizes a team change by 2.5 nats
and a live/dead change by 20 nats. The two-second control rule follows decoding. The writer masks
all ungraded frames to X. Centered features and bidirectional temporal context make this an offline
method, not a causal live system.

## Limitations and verified runtime status

Provider label conventions differ; Metrica labels are approximate event-derived intervals. Tracking
loss, duplicated slots, identity switches and interpolation can distort proximity and motion.
The control rule can suppress real short possessions. Global decoding can carry boundary effects;
half-time positions are masked, but the decoder is invoked on the full timeline. Cache files lack
source-provenance validation and must be isolated for a new dataset.

Prior local validation passed XML structure, match shapes, ordering, half boundaries, all twelve
artifact loads, small synthetic forward passes, compiled decoding and ten lightweight tests. The
existing CSV passed official format validation and explicit ungraded masking checks. The audited
inference took 300.91 seconds. These establish runtime/file compatibility, not possession accuracy.
Exact byte-for-byte repeatability of this CSV has not yet been demonstrated on the audited machine.

## Reproduction and compliance

Use the pretrained models and the [reproduction instructions](reproduction.md). Do not overwrite the
validated CSV. Python 3.14 and dependencies are pinned by `uv.lock`. LightGBM seeds, TCN seeds,
deterministic PyTorch settings, evaluation mode and CPU prediction are implemented. OS newline
translation and hardware/library differences still require an actual byte comparison.

The current source trains exclusively through external-match loaders. This audit did not inspect
competition data or predictions, run ground-truth evaluation, retrain or tune models. Restricted
data and derived predictions must stay local and outside the public repository. Human annotation,
manual output corrections, competition-ground-truth tuning and sharing provided data with AI
services are excluded from this workflow. Historical compliance cannot be certified from source
alone; enrollment terms remain authoritative.
